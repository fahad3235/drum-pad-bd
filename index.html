🥁 Drum Pad BD

Tap the pads or play a 16-step beat — all sounds are synthesized live in your browser. No downloads, no account.

Drum Pads

Keys: A S D F G H J K play the 8 pads. First tap starts audio (browsers require a tap before sound).

Beat Controls

16-Step Sequencer

Tap a square to add or remove that drum on that step. Beat 1 of each bar is outlined. Your pattern is saved automatically.

Record & Play Back

Record your own pad performance, then play it back. Recordings live only in this browser.

    Drum Pad BD is a personal practice toy: sounds are synthesized with the Web Audio API on your device — nothing is uploaded and no audio files are downloaded. Timing depends on your device and browser; it is not a certified instrument or studio tool.

    Built offline-first: this page works with no network connection once loaded, and your pattern and recordings stay in this browser's localStorage.
